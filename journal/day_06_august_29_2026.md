## 🧠 What I Learned
- **A grain is the definition of one row in a table.** First thing to establish before doing normalization and modeling!
  - `Grain` ➔ `Fact` ➔ `Dimension` ➔ `Relationships` ➔ `Validate`
- **GitHub is a storage and code dump tool with organized checking and versioning.** As we are currently on the free version of Databricks, it serves as a top-notch effective collaboration tool.
  - `COMMIT` (edit), `PUSH` (save the edit), `MERGE` (load a draft to the final edit), `PULL` (load the final edits),
- **Documentation is the tracking and note-taking of changes in our work** (definitely as important as our code and pipelines!). 
  - `README / Overview` • `Architecture` • `Data Model` • `Validation` • `Decisions`
